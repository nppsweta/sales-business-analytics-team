
# Profitability Analysis Plan

## Project
Sales & Business Performance Analytics

## Objective
Analyze profit across regions, product categories, sub-categories,
and customer segments to identify profitable areas and potential
business improvement opportunities.

## 1. Overall Profitability
- Calculate total profit across all records.
- Calculate overall profit margin using total profit divided by total sales.
- Establish a baseline for profitability analysis.

## 2. Regional Profitability
- Calculate total profit by region.
- Compare profitability across the four regions.
- Identify regions with the highest and lowest total profit.

## 3. Category Profitability
- Calculate total profit by product category.
- Compare profit across Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology.
- Identify categories contributing most to overall profit.

## 4. Sub-Category Profitability
- Calculate total profit by sub-category.
- Identify sub-categories with negative total profit.
- Investigate low-profit areas for potential improvement.

## 5. Customer Segment Profitability
- Compare total profit across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office.
- Examine differences in sales and profit contribution.

## 6. Tools
- SQL: Aggregate and compare profitability metrics.
- Python and Pandas: Validate calculations and explore patterns.
- Power BI: Visualize profitability through charts and KPI cards.

## 7. Validation
Compare total profit calculated using SQL, Python, and Power BI.
Investigate any discrepancies before reporting findings.

## Expected Deliverables
- Overall profitability summary
- Regional and category profit comparisons
- Sub-category profitability analysis
- Evidence-based business insights and recommendations
