
# Sales Analysis Plan

## Project
Sales & Business Performance Analytics

## Objective
Analyze sales performance to identify patterns across regions,
product categories, customer segments, and time periods.

## 1. Overall Sales Performance
- Calculate total sales across the dataset.
- Establish overall sales performance as a baseline.
- Compare sales across different business dimensions.

## 2. Regional Sales Analysis
- Group sales by region.
- Compare the performance of the four regions.
- Identify the regions with the highest and lowest total sales.
- Examine state-level sales differences where useful.

## 3. Category and Sub-Category Analysis
- Calculate sales by category.
- Compare sales across product sub-categories.
- Identify categories and sub-categories contributing the most sales.

## 4. Customer Segment Analysis
- Compare sales across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
- Identify differences in sales contribution between segments.

## 5. Time-Based Sales Analysis
- Convert Order Date to a proper date type for analysis.
- Compare sales by month and year.
- Identify periods with higher or lower sales.

## 6. Tools and Approach
- SQL: Aggregate and compare sales data.
- Python and Pandas: Calculate metrics and examine patterns.
- Power BI: Present results through charts, KPI cards, and filters.

## 7. Expected Output
- Overall sales summary.
- Regional sales comparison.
- Category and sub-category performance analysis.
- Customer segment comparison.
- Monthly and yearly sales trends.

## Status
Sales analysis requirements documented.
Actual findings will be added after the analysis is completed
and the results have been validated.
