
# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

## Project
Sales & Business Performance Analytics

## Objective
Define the key metrics used to evaluate sales performance
and profitability using the cleaned Superstore dataset.

## 1. Total Sales
**Definition:** Sum of all sales values.

**Formula:** Total Sales = SUM(Sales)

**Purpose:** Measures the overall sales generated in the dataset.

## 2. Total Profit
**Definition:** Sum of all profit values.

**Formula:** Total Profit = SUM(Profit)

**Purpose:** Measures the overall profit or loss across transactions.

## 3. Total Quantity Sold
**Definition:** Sum of the quantity values.

**Formula:** Total Quantity = SUM(Quantity)

**Purpose:** Measures the total quantity of items sold.

## 4. Profit Margin
**Definition:** Profit expressed as a percentage of sales.

**Formula:** Profit Margin = (Total Profit / Total Sales) × 100

**Purpose:** Helps assess profitability relative to sales.

## 5. Sales by Region
**Definition:** Total sales grouped by region.

**Formula:** SUM(Sales), grouped by Region

**Purpose:** Compares sales performance across regions.

## 6. Sales by Category
**Definition:** Total sales grouped by product category.

**Formula:** SUM(Sales), grouped by Category

**Purpose:** Identifies categories contributing the most sales.

## 7. Profit by Sub-Category
**Definition:** Total profit grouped by product sub-category.

**Formula:** SUM(Profit), grouped by Sub-Category

**Purpose:** Identifies high-profit and loss-making sub-categories.

## 8. Monthly Sales
**Definition:** Total sales grouped by month and year.

**Formula:** SUM(Sales), grouped by year and month of Order Date

**Purpose:** Helps identify sales trends over time.
