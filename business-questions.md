
# Business Questions

## Project
Sales & Business Performance Analytics

## Objective
Understand sales and profitability patterns using the
Superstore cleaned dataset.

## Key Questions

1. Which region generates the highest total sales?
2. Which region generates the highest total profit?
3. Which product category generates the most sales?
4. Which sub-categories have low or negative profit?
5. How do sales change month by month?
6. How do annual sales compare from 2014 to 2017?
7. Which states generate the highest sales?
8. How do sales differ across customer segments?
9. Which categories contribute most to overall profit?
10. What business improvements are supported by the findings?

## Planned Tools
- SQL for querying and aggregation
- Python and Pandas for analysis and validation
- Microsoft Power BI for dashboards

## Status
Questions defined; analysis results will be added
after the team completes the relevant queries.
